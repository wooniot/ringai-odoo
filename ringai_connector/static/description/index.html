RingAI Voice Connector

Your AI phone assistant in Odoo — calls, AI summaries and automatic call-back tasks

What it does

  • Call log in Odoo — every inbound or outbound RingAI call as a record: caller, AI summary, transcript, duration and direction.
  • Automatic follow-up — a call that needs follow-up becomes a call-back activity on the right contact.
  • Per company — each Odoo company connects to its own RingAI tenant; multi-company out of the box.
  • AI feedback in your CRM — each call's summary sits next to the customer.

External service & data

This module is a connector to the RingAI voice service and requires an active RingAI account with a per-company connector key to show data. Set the base URL under Settings → RingAI and the connector key per company under Settings → Companies.

Data sent: the connector sends only the connector key over HTTPS to authenticate, and reads back that tenant's calls (caller number/name, summary, transcript, duration). No database access is required. You keep ownership of your data at all times.

Requirements

  • An active RingAI account with a connector key per company.
  • Python requests on the Odoo server.
